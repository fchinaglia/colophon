The register is also compiled by the model about its own contribution. That is the structural weak point, and it is written down rather than hidden.

It is said first because it is the strongest criticism that can be made of a voluntary disclosure, and it is a fair one. The value is not in the proof — it is in the responsibility an author takes on by publishing the record, and in the fact that it can be inspected.

Both measurements are real: a 337-word post and a 1,096-word article, each annotated span by span, by one annotator who is an interested party.
