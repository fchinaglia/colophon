# Why I publish a colophon

Every text I publish now carries a note saying how it was made. Not a badge, not a disclaimer bolted on at the end: a note that points at a record anyone can open.

The reason is narrow. I cannot prove that a text is mine, and neither can anyone else — a finished page carries no trace of the hands that shaped it. What I can do is keep the trace while the shaping happens, and hand it over.

The record does not settle the question of authorship. It moves the question from what I claim to what I am willing to show.
