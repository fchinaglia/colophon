Verify a Colophon case

Everything runs in this page, on your machine. Nothing is uploaded, and it works with no network at all.

This is a convenience copy, and it is not the one that travels. A case carries its own verifier inside it — that copy is what a reader who was handed a document already has, and it is the one to use if you would rather not trust a page served by us.

The two differ in chrome and palette only. What they check is the same code, built from the same source, and both digests are published.

Drop a case folder here

or a bundle.tar, or the signed .pdf that carries one, or the files themselves

What this page does not do. It does not recompute the measurement. It checks that kpi.json, spans.json and annotation.json carry the digests the register sealed — which proves the published numbers are the sealed numbers — and leaves the arithmetic to measure.py, so there is exactly one implementation of it.

It also cannot validate a timestamp authority's certificate, or confirm a Bitcoin anchor: both need the network, and this page never touches it. The commands are shown where they apply.
